What is Freediving?

Freediving is an elite form of underwater diving that relies on breath-holding rather than heavy scuba gear. Experience absolute silence, deep mental relaxation, and harmony with Philippine marine life.

About Freediving in the Philippines

The Philippines is globally recognized as a premier freediving destination, boasting unmatched marine biodiversity, warm tropical waters, and plunging coral walls from Batangas to Panglao and Davao.

Core Freediving Disciplines

Static Apnea (STA)

Holding your breath while floating face down in water. Tests mental relaxation and oxygen conservation.

Constant Weight (CWT)

Descending and ascending using fins or monofin with a constant amount of ballast weight.

Free Immersion (FIM)

Pulling yourself down and up along a descent line without fins, focusing purely on equalisation.

Safety & Professional Training

Safety is paramount in freediving. Always dive with a certified buddy or instructor. Professional training ensures you master proper equalisation techniques (Frenzel and Valsalva), rescue protocols, and safe surface protocols.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Do I need to know how to swim? +

Basic swimming proficiency is required for safety, but introductory courses welcome beginners with patient instructor supervision in shallow confined water.

2. What should I bring to my session? +

Bring comfortable swimwear, a rash guard, towel, personal tumbler for hydration, and cash for local municipal eco-fees.

3. How does Klook external booking work? +

For participating schools with Klook integration, you can scan their custom QR code or click 'Book Through Klook' to finalize your reservation directly on Klook.

4. What payment methods are supported for direct website booking? +

We support GCash, Maya, and direct Philippine bank transfers (BPI / BDO) with manual receipt verification by the school.

5. What is the cancellation and refund policy? +

Cancellations made at least 48 hours before the scheduled dive date are eligible for rescheduling or partial refund, subject to individual school terms.
